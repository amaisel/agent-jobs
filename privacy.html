Agent jobs: privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

Agent jobs is a private, personal application. It is used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account.

What it accesses

The app asks for one Google permission, drive.file. With it, the app can see and change only the files and folders that the app itself creates in the signed-in user's Google Drive. It cannot read any other file in Drive, and it requests no access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service.

What it stores

The only Google data stored is an OAuth refresh token, kept in a file readable only by the developer's own server account. The documents the app creates stay in Google Drive.

Sharing and use

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it for anything other than saving the documents it creates. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and contact

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on the app's Google consent screen.
